Privacy policy

Wingtip Maps · in effect from 8 October 2026

Wingtip Maps ("we") is a VFR flight planner. This page says what the app keeps about you, where, and what leaves it. You can use the map and plan a route without an account; most of what follows is about the account you may make.

What we keep on our server, for your account

  • Who you are: your email address and your display name, and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the identifier that provider gives us for you. We do not receive a password: we have none.
  • Email sign-in: the link we email you is stored as a one-way hash (SHA-256), never as the link itself. It works once and for 15 minutes.
  • Your sign-in session (so you stay signed in for up to 30 days of not using the app).
  • What you save: your airplanes (tail number and performance figures), your flights and their checkpoints, your logbook entries, currency and training records and endorsements, each flight's risk assessment, and the notes you write on checkpoints for yourself.
  • Flown tracks, as GPS points, only if you choose to save a track to a flight.

We keep these until you delete them, or delete your account. We do not sell them or use them for advertising, and there is no analytics or advertising tracker in the app.

Error reports

When the app hits an error on your device that it did not expect, it sends us the error's message and where in the app's code it happened, the page's address without anything after a "?", and your browser's name and version, so we can put it right. It is not linked to your account, and we keep it 30 days.

What stays on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage for your settings (theme, layout, map layers, tips already seen), the charts you kept for offline use, your flown tracks and your drill progress, and your last known position if you have turned on the location arrow. A service worker keeps the app and the chart tiles you have looked at so they open without a connection. None of this is sent to us unless it says so here.

Your location

The location arrow on the map asks your device for your position, only after you turn it on and your device allows it. It is used on the device to draw your airplane and to measure from it. When you ask for the nearest airports, your position is sent to our planner to find them; it is not saved to your account. Turn it off with the arrow, or in your device's settings.

Who else receives anything

  • Anthropic (the Claude model). When the narrative of a briefing is written, the planner sends Claude the text of a request: the departure and destination identifiers, the cruising altitude and the planner's reasoning for it, and each leg's checkpoints, headings, distances, times, fuel and airspeeds, with the text of similar past briefings. It does not send your name, email, account, logbook or position. Anthropic handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Our email sender, which delivers the sign-in link to your address.
  • Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them; they tell us your name, email and identifier, and know that you signed in.
  • The US Census Bureau, if you type a street address in Nearest's field: our server, not your device, asks its geocoder where the address is, to find the airports near it. We do not store it, but a place you pick stays in the page's address (its name and coordinates), so it is in your browser history and in any link you copy, and what you type is in our server's request logs.
  • The FAA and NOAA. Our servers, not your device, ask the FAA's data sets and charts, the Aviation Weather Center and the FAA's TFR list for the data in a briefing. They see our server, not you.
  • Our chart host. Your device fetches chart tiles from us or from the chart tile host we name in the app, which sees your address as any website does.
  • Links to ForeFlight, 1800wxbrief.com, the FAA and others open their own sites, under their own policies, when you tap them.

Deleting your data

You can delete an airplane, a flight, a logbook entry or a saved track in the app at any time. Delete account, in the menu at the top of the console under Sign out, removes your account and everything above on our server, signs you out on every device, and clears the app's settings on the device you use. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes what is on your device.

Children

Wingtip Maps is for pilots and student pilots, and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes. Questions: the Support page.
