# Growth

Pilots pick their tools from their instructor, their club and other pilots, not from ads. Growth here is making the app easy to recommend, quick to prove itself, and worth keeping, then measuring each of those.

## 1. Know the funnel
Write it down for the change at hand, with today's number at each step where there is one:
1. **Found:** App Store page views, links followed, codes redeemed.
2. **First value:** a route planned with its checkpoints and nav log. Target: under two minutes from first open, with no account.
3. **Signed in:** only when a feature needs it (saved flights, a subscription).
4. **Paying:** trial started, trial converted, monthly against yearly.
5. **Kept:** renewed after a year; still planning a route each month.
6. **Brought someone:** a shared route, a live track, an instructor code used.
Fix the step with the biggest drop first.

## 2. Channels, in the order they work for pilots
1. **Instructors and flight schools:** a free instructor account, a code for their students, the mock oral's instructor sign-off, the school dashboard.
2. **Clubs and FBOs:** shared airplane records and booking bring a whole club; FBO listings bring pilots looking for fuel.
3. **What pilots share:** a route link, a live track for family, the kneeboard card, the ForeFlight hand-off. Every shared thing opens the app's own page, not a store wall.
4. **The App Store:** a listing written for searches pilots make ("VFR checkpoints", "student pilot cross-country", "Part 107 airspace"), screenshots of the map and the nav log at iPhone size (the `iphone-review` skill), Apple offer codes for schools and events.
5. **Where pilots talk:** r/flying, Pilots of America, YouTube cross-country walkthroughs, EAA AirVenture and local fly-ins. Show the work; never post as someone else.

## 3. Measure it without overreaching
- Read `docs/privacy-inventory.md` first: collect only what it allows, never a pilot's routes or position for marketing, and say so in the privacy policy.
- Count events on the server where the app already handles them (a route planned, a trial started) rather than adding a tracking SDK.
- Store figures come from App Store Connect and Google Play Console; subscription states from the server's own entitlement records.
- Compare before and after a change over the same weekdays; small numbers swing, so report counts with the period, not only percentages.

## 4. Experiments
One change at a time, with the step it should move, the number it should reach, and the date it is judged. Prices and trials change only through the store's own offer tools, and never for a subscriber mid-term.

## Report
The funnel with today's numbers, the step chosen and why, the change, how it will be measured and when, and what it needs from the owner (store accounts, a code campaign, a post under their own name).
